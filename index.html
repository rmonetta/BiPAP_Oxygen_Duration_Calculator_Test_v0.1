Planning estimate only. This calculator estimates oxygen duration for pre-transport planning. It uses a conservative height-based tidal-volume estimate, a fixed respiratory rate, assumed mask-leak conditions, a 3 L/min HAMILTON-T1 base flow, and a 300 PSI cylinder reserve. Actual minute ventilation, patient effort, mask leak, FiO₂ requirements, and oxygen consumption may vary. Confirm adequate oxygen supply before departure and continuously monitor ventilator and cylinder parameters during transport.
