i

Planning estimate only. This calculator is intended for pre-arrival transport planning using the Hamilton T1 ventilator. Estimated oxygen duration is calculated using conservative planning assumptions for patient size, respiratory rate, tidal volume, and anticipated mask seal. Actual oxygen consumption may vary based on patient effort, ventilator settings, mask fit, circuit leak, and changes in patient condition. Always verify adequate oxygen supply before departure and continuously monitor the patient, ventilator, and oxygen source throughout transport.
